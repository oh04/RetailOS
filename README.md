# RetailOS

RetailOS is a store management application for tracking products and stock quantities.
It will help employees check inventory and managers keep stock information up to date.

## Users
- Employees can view products and available quantities.
- Managers will be able to add products and update inventory.

## First feature
Display a list of products with their current stock quantities.

## Sample products
- Whole Milk: 24 units
- Wheat Bread: 15 units
- Paper Towels: 32 units
